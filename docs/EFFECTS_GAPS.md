## Measured summary

- In the first CI run, geometry matched Chromium on all 25 original effects probes (mean IoU 1.0000, max edge 0.00 px). This does **not** predict the remeasured `z-index-negative` result after changing that probe.
- The first CI shows **six unpainted effects** in their probes: `filter` brightness (6.48%), grayscale (6.47%), blur (2.50%), drop-shadow (1.71%), and `mix-blend-mode` screen (6.48%) and multiply (4.51%). These percentages differ because painted regions and the comparison threshold (>16/255 per RGB channel) differ, **not** because multiply or other effects have partial support. Clip-path circle has a distinct radius-basis discrepancy (1.29%), despite clipping to a circle.
- **Low whole-image pixel percentages are not evidence of correct painting.** In `opacity-nested` (0.78% changed), 2,575 pixels differ, including 2,177 with channel error >64/255; the missing overflowed child paint is mostly **outside** `#probe`'s border box. Other original probes below 1% require visual inspection before claiming parity. The old `z-index-negative` 0.00% was non-diagnostic: Chromium also hid the target behind the stage background; its updated isolated version awaits CI.
- WPT delta for this PR: 0 newly passing, 0 newly failing (probes and export keys only). No WPT area is selected for compositing or filter-effects (NOT_SELECTED).
- Phase-2 priority: **opacity group overflow/descendant clipping**; the **four unpainted filter effects** (drop-shadow, blur, grayscale, brightness); the **two unpainted blend modes** (multiply, screen); then **clip-path circle percentage-radius basis** (normalised diagonal, not width). Prioritisation is based on first-run pixel evidence, not proven upstream root causes.
